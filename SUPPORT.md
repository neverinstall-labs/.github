# Support

These tools are provided as is, without a support agreement or response-time commitment.

- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue in the tool's repository. Maintainers review issues weekly.
- **Security problems:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **Neverinstall products:** contact us through [neverinstall.com](https://neverinstall.com).

Questions about another vendor's platform are welcome when they relate to a tool here. For problems with that
platform itself, contact its vendor.
